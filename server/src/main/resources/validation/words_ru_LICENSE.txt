Dictionary extension (November 2026)
------------------------------------
This is a modified version. It combines the original Hunspell-derived list
with lower-case single-word Russian dictionary entries and inflected forms
from OpenRussian.org, by the OpenRussian contributors.

OpenRussian snapshot:
https://github.com/Badestrand/russian-dictionary/tree/50e210c4803237779cb562bc1abcea529066031c
Source tables: nouns.csv, adjectives.csv, verbs.csv and others.csv.
The adaptation extracts Russian headwords and explicit forms, strips stress
marks, folds ё to е and deduplicates. No translations are included.
